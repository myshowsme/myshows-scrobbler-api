Follow these rules. They keep traffic at a reasonable level and make it easier to
investigate problems if they come up.

::field-group
  ::field{name="No more than one /pause every 10 seconds" type="per active session"}
  Going faster gains nothing: the position still changes less often than the user moves it.
  ::

  ::field{name="Skip ticks without movement" type="saves requests"}
  If `progress` hasn't changed since the last request, there's no need to send one.
  ::

  ::field{name="Set source_app and app_version" type="required in practice"}
  This is the only way for MyShows to tell your app's traffic apart from others, including
  when a problem needs investigating rather than blocking.
  ::

  ::field{name="Don't polish progress" type="honest data"}
  Send what the player actually reports.
  ::
::
